# CLSEPA Petition Database — Staff Guide

This guide is for CLSEPA legal staff who will be uploading and managing petition decisions.

## Logging In

1. Go to the database website and click **Staff Login** in the top right
2. Enter your email and password
3. You'll be redirected to the Admin Panel

## Uploading a New Decision

1. Click the **Upload Decision** tab
2. **Select the PDF file** — click the file picker and choose the decision PDF
3. **Fill in the details:**
   - **City** — Select East Palo Alto or Mountain View
   - **Case Number** — e.g., 2024-0001 or C22230055
   - **Address** — Property address
   - **Unit** — Apartment/unit number (if applicable)
   - **Petitioner Name** — Tenant name(s)
   - **Respondent Name** — Landlord/property management name
   - **Hearing Date** — Date of the hearing
   - **Decision Date** — Date the decision was issued
   - **Decision Type** — Select from dropdown (Hearing Officer Decision, Appeal, etc.)
   - **Hearing Officer** — Name of the hearing officer
   - **Amount Awarded** — Dollar amount, if applicable
   - **Outcome Summary** — Brief description of what was decided
4. **Select keyword tags** — Click all categories that apply to this decision (e.g., Mold, Plumbing, Heat). These are what tenants will search by.
5. Click **Upload Decision**
6. The decision is now live and searchable by the public

## Managing Existing Decisions

1. Click the **Manage Decisions** tab
2. You'll see a table of all uploaded decisions
3. To delete a decision, click **Delete** in its row (this cannot be undone)

## Managing Keywords / Categories

Keywords are the issue categories that tenants search by. They should match the categories on your petition intake form.

1. Click the **Keywords** tab
2. **To add a keyword:** Type the name (e.g., "Pest Infestation") and click **Add**
3. **To remove a keyword:** Click the **x** next to it

### Suggested Keywords (from petition form categories)
- Mold
- Plumbing
- Electrical
- Heat / Heating
- Rent Overcharge
- Habitability
- Code Violations
- Pest Infestation
- Water (hot/cold)
- Appliance (stove, refrigerator)
- Structural (floors, walls, ceiling)
- Common Area Maintenance
- Security

## What Tenants See

When a tenant visits the database, they see:
1. **Search filters** — dropdown for issue category, city, decision type, and a text search box
2. **Decision cards** — showing address, city, case number, date, amount awarded, and keyword tags
3. **Decision detail page** — full metadata plus a button to view the original PDF

Tenants cannot upload, edit, or delete anything. They can only search and read.
